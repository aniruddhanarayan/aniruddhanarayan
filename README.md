## <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/◈-ABOUT-8b1a1a?style=flat-square" height="22"/> About Me

I'm **Aniruddha Narayan** — Founder and CEO of ![Aminuteman Technologies](https://img.shields.io/badge/Aminuteman%20Technologies-8b1a1a?style=flat-square&labelColor=2a0a0a), a bootstrapped defense-tech company building ![AI-driven UAV Systems](https://img.shields.io/badge/AI--driven%20UAV%20%26%20Autonomous%20Systems-2c4a6e?style=flat-square&labelColor=0a1520) to protect soldiers and strengthen global defense sovereignty.

When I started studying defense technology, one truth stood out: ![systems are decades behind](https://img.shields.io/badge/systems%20are%20decades%20behind-4a2f1a?style=flat-square&labelColor=2a1808). And when technology fails, people pay the price.

At ![Age 18](https://img.shields.io/badge/age%2018-8b1a1a?style=flat-square&labelColor=2a0a0a), I decided to change that. Starting from college labs, I assembled a ![22-member engineering team](https://img.shields.io/badge/22--member%20engineering%20team-2c4a6e?style=flat-square&labelColor=0a1520) united by one idea:

> Build from first principles. Build technology that can bear the risks humans shouldn't.

Our mission is to establish ![AI Brain Sovereignty](https://img.shields.io/badge/AI%20Brain%20Sovereignty-3a3a48?style=flat-square&labelColor=1a1a24) — building systems where technology bears the highest risk, so more defenders come home safely.
