## 🧠 About Me

I'm **Aniruddha Narayan** — Founder & CEO of **Aminuteman Technologies**, a bootstrapped defense-tech company building AI-driven UAV and autonomous systems to **protect soldiers** and **strengthen global defense sovereignty**.

When I started studying defense technology, one truth stood out: **systems are decades behind**. And when technology fails, people pay the price.

At **18**, I decided to change that. Starting from college labs, I assembled a **22-member engineering team** united by one idea:

> *Build from first principles. Build technology that can bear the risks humans shouldn't.*

Our mission is to **establish AI Brain sovereignty** — building systems where technology bears the highest risk, so more defenders come home safely.
